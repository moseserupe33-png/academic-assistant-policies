About Career App

Career App is a mobile application designed exclusively for students in Kenya who have completed their KCSE examinations. The app provides a user-friendly platform that helps students discover higher education courses—including degree, diploma, certificate, and artisan programs—that they qualify for based on their KCSE results and cluster points.

The app aims to simplify the career pathway decision process by providing a clear, centralized hub for course requirements, university cut-off trends, and institution options. Career App is developed with performance, accuracy, and accessibility in mind to ensure every student can confidently navigate their next academic step on their mobile devices.

The app is continually updated with official placement guidelines and user feedback to ensure accuracy, simplicity, and reliability in guiding Kenyan students toward their ideal careers.
